For CBSE Class 10, students typically study 5 main subjects, though many schools also offer an optional 6th subject.

Many schools also offer an additional (6th) subject, such as:

Information Technology (IT)
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Computer Applications
Home Science
Painting
Other Skill Education subjects
